# Build brand material with ZAUQ

Posters, ads, social, email, decks, covers — anything that carries the mark.
Read `guidelines/brand-guidelines.md` first; this is the working recipe.

## 1. Pick the canvas

| Piece | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Feed ad, square | 1080 × 1080 | headline 88px |
| Feed ad, portrait | 1080 × 1350 | **the default** |
| Story / reel cover | 1080 × 1920 | copy block low |
| OG / social card | 1200 × 630 | ceremonial lockup, centred |
| Poster | any, ink ground | scale all specs off the width |
| Email | 600 wide | cream ground, ink wordmark |
| Deck slide | 1920 × 1080 | ink ground, 9% margins |

All px specs below are **per 1080 of canvas width** — scale linearly.

## 2. Ground it

Ink `#0B0A09`, flat. No gradient as a background, ever. Cream `#F2EDE3` grounds
are for print stationery and email only. Film grain over the whole composition.

## 3. Place the image

One photograph, following the imagery direction in the brand guide: one warm
directional spotlight, near-black ground, brass and cream accents, film grain,
deliberate negative space where the headline will sit. Behind the copy block,
add a **feathered ink scrim** — solid under the words, fading into the image.
Never darken the whole frame.

## 4. Set the copy block

Left-aligned at **9% of the width**; anchored 11% up from the bottom (default)
or 16% down from the top.

```
Headline   Bodoni Moda 500 · 104px (88 on square) · line-height 1.06 · tracking −1
           2–3 hand-broken lines, 2–5 words each, cream
Accent     exactly ONE of those lines in gold #D8B26A, italic
  6px
Subline    Archivo 400 · 30px · #B9AE97 · one sentence
  30px
CTA        gold bar, radius 4, height 54 · Archivo 700 21px · tracking 2 · ink, uppercase
```

Write the headline as a spoken line, not a slogan: *"A full closet. / Nothing
to wear."* — the second line gold italic. Then the subline explains, once:
*"ZAUQ styles what you already own."*

## 5. Add the mark

The wordmark sits **opposite** the copy block, at the same 9% margin, in cream
or gold, kerned ZA .24em / AU .20em / UQ .16em, above its 72px minimum, clear
space respected. For ceremonial pieces use the stacked lockup: mark, wordmark,
a 3px gold rule one twelfth of the canvas wide, and the tagline in Archivo 600
at 0.32em tracking.

Assets: `assets/brand/zauq-wordmark-{cream,gold,ink}.png`,
`assets/brand/zauq-mark-{cream,ink}.png`, `assets/brand/favicon.svg`.

## 6. Copy rules

Second person, British spelling, short verbless lines, full stops instead of
exclamation marks, real apostrophes, specific numbers, no emoji. Reuse the
lexicon: *the brief, the rail, the niche, the mirror, the ledger, already
waiting, let it go*.

## 7. Check before you export

1. Ink ground, one warm light, no cool tones, no high-key white.
2. Exactly one gold italic line in the headline.
3. 9% margins on all copy; nothing centred over a photograph.
4. Wordmark opposite the copy, above minimum, clear space clean.
5. Gold `#D8B26A` for brand material — not product brass `#C8A45E`.
6. Grain on; no drop shadows; radius 3 (4 on the CTA bar).
7. No emoji, no icon set, no third typeface.
