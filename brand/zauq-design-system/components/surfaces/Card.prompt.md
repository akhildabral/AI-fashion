The default container for grouped content.

```jsx
<Card hover style={{ padding: 'var(--space-4)' }}>
  <p style={{ fontFamily: 'var(--font-display)', fontSize: 'var(--text-display-6)' }}>Goes with 7 of your pieces</p>
</Card>
```

Resting cards have NO shadow — that is a hard rule in this system. If a card looks flat, the fix is the hairline, the raised fill and the film grain, not elevation.
