The only slider in ZAUQ — a 2px brass thread with a rectangular 18×26 thumb, from the fitting.

```jsx
<Tape min={0} max={10} value={warmth} onChange={(e) => setWarmth(+e.target.value)} label="Warmth" />
```

The fill left of the thumb is brass, right of it is 12% ink. Do not add a track background or a round thumb.
