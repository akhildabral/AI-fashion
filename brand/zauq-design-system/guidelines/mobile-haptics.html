select()selectiona detent, a taste choice, a day change

thud()impact mediuma destructive action fired

One per user action, in the same frame as the visual, and never the only feedback. Fire-and-forget, safe on hardware without a motor. Reduced-motion users still get haptics; the animation is what backs off.
